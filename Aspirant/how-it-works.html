How It Works

A simple, seamless process. No apps to download and no accounts to create.

  1. We Design We create a custom-branded frame for your specific event.
  2. You Receive We deliver a unique QR code directly to you.
  3. Guests Snap Event guests scan the QR code and take their photos.
  4. Guests Save The branded photos are saved directly to their own devices.
