6. The Booth Frame Service — How It Works and What We Collect

If you contact us to enquire about the booth frame service:[cite: 2]

  • All enquiries are handled directly by the DNA Media team through WhatsApp or email.[cite: 2] No personal information is collected through this website itself.[cite: 2]
  • During the enquiry and service process, you will share contact and event details with us.[cite: 2] We use this information only to deliver the service you have requested.[cite: 2] It is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes and is not sold.[cite: 2]
  • We agree an expiry date for your frame at the time of delivery.[cite: 2] After that date, the QR code becomes inactive and the frame is no longer accessible.[cite: 2]

For guests who use the booth at your event:[cite: 2]

  • Guests scan the QR code and access the booth through their browser.[cite: 2] The branded frame is applied using technology that runs entirely on each guest's own device.[cite: 2]
  • Photos are saved directly to each guest's own device.[cite: 2] DNA Media does not receive, store, access, or retain copies of those photos.[cite: 2]
  • No biometric data, facial recognition data, or personally identifiable information derived from any guest's face is collected, processed, or transmitted to DNA Media or any of its service providers through the standard booth experience.[cite: 2]
